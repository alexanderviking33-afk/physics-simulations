LIVE Watch the fission chain reaction
Uranium atoms (blue circles) sit inside the fuel rods. A fast neutron is a small yellow dot moving quickly. When it passes through the graphite moderator it slows down visibly — the dot grows larger and starts to crawl. Those slow neutrons are the ones that split uranium atoms, releasing new fast neutrons. The control rods absorb neutrons.
